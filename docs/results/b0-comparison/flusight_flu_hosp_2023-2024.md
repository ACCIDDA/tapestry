# Influenza admissions · 2023-2024

[Canonical report and model names](../b0-configuration-comparison.md)

All models use the same frozen tasks. US is the native national prediction; states/DC are evaluated individually. Fans connect the four horizons from one forecast origin. Blue bands show 50%/95% intervals around the median; black curves show truth. Every fourth origin is illustrated. Admissions are counts; ED visits are proportions.
