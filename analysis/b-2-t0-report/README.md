# Completed B-2 T-0 report

The requested runs are interpreted as postprocessing the completed `b-2-t0` training sweep, not retraining it. All 927 runs (309 configurations × seeds 42, 43, 44) were verified complete on Longleaf on 2026-09-30. `build_report.py` uses the standard planner ranking, plotting, and report functions, selecting the three lowest mean combined scores for fans and heatmaps. Report job: 3061288. See `commands.sh` for the plan, original training launch, status/rank and report launch commands.

`analyze.py` reads the final ranking identified in `ranking-path.txt`. It requires exactly 309 configurations with three seeds each. Matched comparisons hold every serialized Scenario field fixed except the stated factor. Seed differences are averaged within each seed across matched settings; a 95% Student-t interval uses the three seed means (2 degrees of freedom). Matched settings are not treated as independent replicates. Context sign counts describe heterogeneity and do not provide independent hypothesis tests.

Intervals describe fitting-seed variability conditional on the fixed CV tasks. They do not quantify uncertainty for new seasons, data revisions, new locations, or selection among 309 candidates. No multiplicity correction or independent post-selection test is claimed. B0 comparisons align seed IDs, but differing architectures and recipes mean these are not identical stochastic trajectories. B0 used 2,048 forecast samples versus 256 in this sweep, and a different training panel/recipe. Both use finalized target histories. The metric uses identical frozen evaluation support, US 20%/states-DC 80%, admissions weight 1/ED 0.5, and equal season weights. Only flu/COVID admissions contribute in 2024–25; all six targets contribute in 2025–26.

Fans use the lowest seed (42) and forecast-distribution 50%/90% bands, not confidence intervals for model performance. Standard heatmaps average location-relative WIS over the three seeds. The supplementary source-by-backbone heatmap holds spatial sharing at none, masking at 0.2, coordinates off, covariate encoder at summary, and signal features at none. Missing combinations are left blank. Images are generated without visual inspection, per project instructions.
